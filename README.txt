SEDIMENT
A todo app where every finished task becomes a star in your sky.

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (your Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
3. Always open it in the same browser, because your tasks are saved in that browser.

TIPS
- Press Enter to add a task, click the circle to finish it.
- Double-click a task to edit it. Press / to jump to the input.
- Finished tasks are never deleted. Click a star to reopen one.
- Open "Archive and backup" at the bottom, click "Show my data", and keep that text somewhere safe.
  To move your tasks to another browser or computer, paste the text and click "Restore from text".
- Avoid clearing site data or cookies for this page, since that also clears saved tasks.
- The fonts load from the internet. Offline, it falls back to your system fonts.
